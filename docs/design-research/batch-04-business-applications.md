## Batch 4 Summary & Key Architectural Insights
- **Employee Directories**: Rippling, Gusto, and Deel prove that a great employee directory needs: 2-line avatar+name+email cells, department tags, active/pending status pills, and right-aligned action menus.
- **Micro-Interactions**: Calendly's instant feedback ("Copied!") and Attio's hairline tactile borders elevate the perceived quality of the software without adding visual clutter.
- **High Information Density**: PostHog and Rippling demonstrate that professional enterprise users prefer high data density (36-40px row heights, monospace IDs, sticky headers) over spacious, hollow cards.
